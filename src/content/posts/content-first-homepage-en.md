# The Homepage: An Entrance, Not a Warehouse

Many blog themes cram all capabilities onto the homepage at once. While it might seem feature-rich, readers often struggle to decide what to read first.

## My Homepage Breakdown

The new homepage is divided into four sections:

1.  Top hero section and brand semantics
2.  Category entry points and status cards
3.  Article stream
4.  Sidebar with author information and indexing capabilities

## Why This Approach is More Durable

Because when new features are added in the future, you only need to determine which layer they belong to, rather than finding a temporary spot to cram them in.

## Benefits for Future APIs

Once the homepage structure is stable, even if you integrate:

-   Popular articles
-   Visit statistics
-   Real-time status

This data will simply replace existing content in its designated place, without disrupting the overall visual order.
